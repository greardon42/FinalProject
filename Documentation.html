Super Bowl Documentation

My Design Process

My design process was kind of simple compared to what imagine it was like for other people as I saw the data and I already knew what I was going to do with my data. I found my data that I was going to use at the website: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/timoboz/superbowl-history-1967-2020 I chose this data as I love the NFL and I wanted to know some of the most winningest franchises and this is the most recent data I could Find for it. I knew the best way to create this visualization was to make a stacked bar chart to show wins and losses for both teams and a pie chart to show percentages to show how dominant some teams were throughout the years and also a line chart to show the trend of points scored over time to see if theres any trends. Those three were my initial designs and I just kept updating them until I could figure it out.

Rationale for Design Choices

My Rationale for making these the two types of charts is becasue I felt they were the two type of charts to truly represent the data. I say this because the bar chart can show both the wins and losses of each team that has been to the superbowl and the pie chart is to show the true differences between teams like the patriots and the titans as the patriots take up a lot of the chart and the panthers take up very little. The colors I chose for the bar graph was green and red as they in my opinion reflect good and negative so that one was a pretty simple choice. I chose to make the pie chart have the same colors as the team and in post I wish I changed that a little bit because some of the names aren't able to be seen. I made the pie chart pop out when you hover over it and say all of the information I also included in the pie chart the appearances each team made as it makes it look way cleaner and much easier to understand. The bar chart has two seperate charts that each show wins and losses in the super bowl. The way I chose to end up cleaning this data is by getting rid of stuff like final scores and the information I did not need as all I needed was wins and losses and points scored so I added those together to make appearances. I made the line chart to mostly see if offenses and defenses have changed scoring wise over time. I added the axis for the bar graph and line graph to be very detailed and show all of the information available.

Final results

In the bar chart I made it to show how each team really competed in the super bowl by showing a bar for wins and losses and it can answer questions like which team has won the most super bowls and which team has lost the most super bowls. This pie chart was to really showcase which teams have appeared the most in the super bowl and how many times they have won and lost as well. You can conclude from this that the patriots steelers and broncos have been there many times but it will not show you how many times they lost like the bar chart it just shows you appearances. The line chart really wasn't meant to show super bowl success or failure it was more meant to show how the trend of scoring has been going on Over the past 60 years or so and you can conclude theres not much of a spike in scoring as I would have thought even though the game has changes so much over time.
